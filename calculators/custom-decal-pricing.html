Formula: (Width × Height ÷ 144) × Qty = total sq ft, summed across all sizes  ·  × the $/sq ft for that band  ·  Minimum: $90 material  ·  Setup: $50 (GRT-50, new art).
Example — 6×(6×6) + 10×(12×12) + 10×(18×18) = 1.5 + 10 + 22.5 = 34 sq ft → 34 × $12 = $408 + $50 setup = $458 before tax.
