House Rules

  1. Bring us your goods, we price by the piece. Price × quantity, with a $75 minimum per job — bill the difference on LTM.
  2. Not on the menu? We quote the time: $25 per quarter hour at the bench (sewing, finishing, hand work) or $37.50 per quarter hour on a machine (embroidery heads, heat press, laser) — bench $100/h, machine $150/h; on DECG, DECC or Laser; Erik or Ruthie approves the estimate.
  3. Supplies we provide (transfers, labels, bags, special thread) are priced the same way as our garments — cost ÷ 0.53, never a garment. Goods you supply are worked at your risk. We supply the garment? Use the quote builder; names, patches and puff are add-ons there at the same prices.
