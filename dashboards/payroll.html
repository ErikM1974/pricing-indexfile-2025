Leave Balances

Current vacation and sick hours for active employees, refreshed from the last payroll packet. A negative balance is not an error — staff accrue their first 40 vacation hours at their one-year anniversary and can run negative until then.

Where vacation accrued or used shows 112 → 80, the first figure is what payroll imported and the second is what the slip prints. Payroll books hours to the tax year of the check date, so vacation taken in the last pay period of a year inflates both accrued and used by the same carryover in the following year. They cancel, which is why remaining is always correct as imported. Sick hours are never adjusted — Washington State sick leave legitimately carries over.
