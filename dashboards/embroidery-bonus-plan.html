Why the two goals are different

Each goal comes from that rep's own book history, not from a company-wide number. Nika's accounts have produced $190K–$247K of Q3 embroidery in recent years. Taneisha's book — inherited from Taylar in August 2025 — has never exceeded $113K in a Q3, going back to 2021.

A single shared number would be unfair in both directions: $235,000 would be out of reach for Taneisha, and $100,000 is something Nika would pass without trying. The percentages are the fair comparison, and the rungs are identical percentages — so identical effort pays identically.

Worth knowing: Taneisha's 115% rung is beating the best Q3 that book has ever produced. These are real targets, not token ones.

Where $740,000 comes from

Q1 was $721,553 and Q2 was $584,346, so $1,305,899 is already banked. If Q4 lands where it did last year ($953,152), then:

$3,000,000 − $1,305,899 − $953,152 = $740,949

That's the whole derivation. Every order counts toward it — every decoration method, every rep, not just embroidery.

4 What counts

  • Custom Embroidery orders — caps included (they used to be a separate order type; they're the same line now).
  • Invoiced subtotal — before tax and shipping. Same basis as your online-store bonus.
  • Invoiced between July 1 and September 30. An order that ships in late September but invoices in October counts toward Q4 — same rule as the online-store bonus.
  • Accounts assigned to you in ShopWorks.

Not counted: webstore orders (they have their own bonus), and contract decorating for other shops.
