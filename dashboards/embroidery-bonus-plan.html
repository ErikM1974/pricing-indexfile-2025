Why the two goals are different

Each goal comes from that rep's own book history — specifically, the average of your last two quarters of embroidery on accounts that count toward this bonus. A single shared number would be unfair in both directions.

Those two quarters were recalculated with webstore accounts taken out, so your goal will look lower than the quarterly totals you remember. Nothing was taken away: your embroidery this quarter is measured the same way, so both sides of the comparison moved together. Filtering the revenue while leaving the old goal in place would have made the target unreachable.

The rate is paid per percentage point, not per dollar, precisely so the bigger book is not worth more. Finish at 110% of your goal and you earn exactly the same as the other rep finishing at 110% of hers.

Your goal is yours alone — built from your own book, not measured against anyone else's. Because the rate pays per percentage point, you and the other rep earn the same amount for the same achievement against your own number.

This one counts everything

The kicker measures all company embroidery — every account, every person, webstore orders included. That's deliberately wider than your individual bonus. It's a team number, so everything the shop embroiders counts toward it, including work that isn't yours.

Embroidery is our biggest line at 46% of everything we sell, which makes it the clearest single lever on the company's $3M year.

4 What counts

  • Custom Embroidery orders — caps included (they used to be a separate order type; they're the same line now).
  • Invoiced subtotal — before tax and shipping. Same basis as your online-store bonus.
  • Invoiced between July 1 and September 30. An order that ships in late September but invoices in October counts toward Q4 — same rule as the online-store bonus.
  • Accounts assigned to you in ShopWorks.
  • Direct orders only — webstore accounts are paid through the online-store commission instead.

Not counted: any account that orders through an InkSoft webstore (those earn you the online-store commission), and contract decorating for other shops.
