The rules that do not bend

  1. One token file. Colour, type, space, radius, shadow, motion and z-index come from tokens.css. A page never types a hex colour; npm run lint:css rejects it.
  2. Colour means a person or a department, not a page type. Steve is green, Ruth is purple, Bradley is slate, the shop floor is blue, the AE dashboard is maroon. Money and quoting is NW green. Two greens and two blues exist on purpose — never merge them.
  3. Reference pages wear the staff chrome, not a person's colour. Training, policies and the handbook use neutral shared chrome with a brand ownership marker and a neutral tile on the staff dashboard. Customer-facing pages use the brand green (--color-brand).
  4. Printed sheets use the print palette. The fillable forms are printed at the counter; their colours were sampled from the official PDFs and stay exact (--print-*).
  5. A page's own colours are declared once. Add a missing color to tokens.css. Migrated pages select supported semantic theme aliases; they never shadow the numeric spacing or radius scale.
  6. No !important without a written reason. A flag carries a stylelint-disable-next-line … -- why comment (the hidden attribute, a reduced-motion kill switch, Caspio markup we do not own, an inline-style precedence a builder still needs); a legacy override stack carries one file-level reason and retires page by page as that page is redesigned. Nothing is exempt.
  7. Tokens load first. The token file declares the cascade layers (reset → tokens → base → components → utilities → overrides); a page stylesheet is linked after it and holds only what is specific to that page.

Building a page

  1. Copy templates/page-template.html; replace the placeholders; one page stylesheet, one page script.
  2. Write the stylesheet with var(--…) names from this page. Need a colour that is not here? Add it to tokens.css with a comment. Put unique arrangements in the scoped components.pages layer.
  3. Run npm run lint:css and npm test. Register the files in ACTIVE_FILES.md. A staff page that should be restricted gets a Staff_Page_Access row.
  4. Give the page the colour family of the person or department it belongs to, and the matching family class on its staff-dashboard tile.

Decisions log

This page is a working document. Every standard that changed how a page looks is recorded here, newest first, with the release that shipped it.

  • 2026-09-08 · shared components, first pilots. Public Sans, neutral staff surfaces, cream customer surfaces, 44px controls and semantic density now share one scoped component layer. Steve, Ruth, Bradley, shop-floor and AE colors remain distinct. Design Queue, Art Billing & File Reference and Company Store Inquiry replace their competing legacy sheets. The rest of the application migrates by family; the component examples above are the maintained reference.
  • 2026-09-07 · palette pass 2 — the storefront and catalog. Reviewed page by page with Erik's authority to make the customer-facing pages look better. Verdict: the 2026 storefront is already a coherent system (cream paper, forest green, rush orange, Bricolage display), so the changes are small and deliberate. The closed sample-cart drawer is now hidden as well as slid away — it used to sit in the scrollbar gutter and stay keyboard-focusable off-screen — and its text uses the storefront inks instead of generic greys. Three accent tints the storefront pages shared are named once (--store-green-300, --store-green-200, --store-rush-tint) and thirteen near-token page colours were folded into the ramps. Kept on purpose: the catalog's colour-family swatches, the hi-vis safety colours, the home hero accents and the customer portal's gold — they stand for products or a chosen accent, not the brand.
  • 2026-09-07 · palette pass 1 — the ramps grow, 604 page colours go. Thirty colours that between eight and thirty-five sheets each declared as their own page variable are now named once as ramp steps (--red-500, --amber-200, --emerald-800, --green-600, --blue-800, --brand-mid…). The two legacy palettes are named for what they are — --bs-* (Bootstrap 4) and --material-green-* — so a redesign can retire them on purpose instead of by accident. Every page variable equal to a token was then removed and its uses rewritten: 147 sheets, 604 variables, no look change (31-page sample pixel-identical). The page variables that remain are the genuinely page-specific colours.
  • 2026-09-07 · the tail, and the dead sheets. The twelve sheets no family covered (the admin pages, production shifts, the price audit report, the employee bundles, the calibration tool, the vendor portals, the DTF prototype) read the token file, pixel-identical. Eighteen stylesheets that no page, script, route or import referenced (about 17,000 lines, several pre-dating the 2026 storefront) were deleted on Erik's instruction, with the two test fixtures that pointed at one of them. Every served stylesheet is now under stylelint and every served page that loads one loads the tokens. Next: the storefront and catalog palette pass — first an app-wide, zero-visual collapse of the page variables that share a value across many sheets into ramp tokens, then the customer-facing pages themselves, with Erik's authority to make them look better.
  • 2026-09-07 · quote builders (the last family). The four quote builders, the monogram form and the fast quote, and the thirteen calculator, dashboard and storefront pages that share their sheets read the token file, and so do the AE dashboard's nav and submit-form sheets (the last of shared_components/css): 2,573 colour uses became 1,415 exact tokens, 691 near tokens and 467 sheet-scoped variables; the shell's --pnw-* forest palette and the common sheet's --builder-* palette now alias the tokens wherever the values match. The generated hash-named quote-builder-inline.css is retired: the same 127 declaration sets live in quote-builder-utilities.css under names that say what they do (.qb-mt-4, .qb-bg-amber-100-p-2-8-r-4-c-amber-800). Their !important stays for now — it reproduces the precedence an inline attribute had over the builders' id-based rules, and dropping it means refactoring those id rules on the money path, which is design work, not a script. Follow-up: retire the flags builder by builder as the id rules are refactored; quote-print.css (296 flags over inline-styled print markup) is the biggest. The sticker pricing page keeps its own 20/24/32 spacing steps, which shadow the token scale on that page.
  • 2026-09-07 · staff pages + customer portal. The forty-five remaining pages under pages/ (art request and mockup detail, quote view, invoices, policies, the customer portal and login, the garment designer, the SanMar vendor portal) and the product page's shared sheet read the token file: 3,324 colour uses became 1,650 exact tokens, 1,023 near tokens and 651 sheet-scoped variables. Six sheets keep their own radius or shadow values that shadow the tokens on their pages (3-day tees, custom tees and caps, quote view, quote audit, the product page, the JDS mockup creator) — recorded, not moved. The icon font (Font Awesome) is exempt from the generic-font-family rule: its glyphs are private-use codepoints and no fallback renders them. Follow-up: seven sheets (invoice, quote view, quote audit, art request detail, policy detail, garment designer, embroidery contract pricing) are override stacks held together by !important (298 flags across the batch, all documented).
  • 2026-09-07 · calculators. The 25 calculator pages read the token file; every colour is a token or a sheet-scoped variable. The DTF, screen-print and universal pricing pages are stacks of fix/override sheets held together by !important (446 flags, all documented); untangling them is per-page design work, not a script — listed here as follow-up. The two sheets the quote builders also load wait for that family.
  • 2026-09-07 · dashboards. Sixty dashboards and the queue-dashboard system they share now read the token file; the person/department colours those pages already wore are code (--art-theme: var(--color-ruth)). Decision approved 2026-09-08, rollout in progress: retain the global numeric scale and use semantic density. Two legacy spacing/radius scales still exist — art-hub.css (4 → 32, radius 4/8/12/16, used by 41 queue pages) and the token file's (4 → 96, radius 6/10/14/20, from the Staff Dashboard seed). Nothing moved: on art-hub pages the page copy still wins. Choosing one scale re-lays out 41 staff pages, so it stays a decision, not a script.
  • 2026-09-07 · storefront + components. The customer-facing 2026 storefront palette (cream paper, forest greens, safety-orange rush accent, Bricolage Grotesque display type) is named as the --store-* tokens and shown on this page beside the staff palette; nwca-2026-core.css aliases to it. The shared component and utility layers (components.css, utilities.css) shipped — new pages get buttons, cards, fields, tables, alerts, badges, modals, toasts and empty states on tokens without writing them.
  • 2026-09-08 · training family. All twenty-six reviewed training and reference pages use Public Sans, a white navigation bar and restrained green accents. Fields, actions, game feedback and keyboard focus follow the shared component system. Procedure guides share checklists and reference layouts; manuals share chapter navigation, mobile contents and complete printing. Training exercises use responsive fields, native dialogs and dependable round/progress state. Page styles own their exercise or document arrangements; the superseded training header sheet has been retired.
  • 2026-09-07 · forms family. The printable forms keep their PDF-sampled palette as the --print-* tokens; two stray inks (#333, #666) were folded into the print inks.
  • 2026-09-07 · foundation. One app-wide token file promoted from the staff dashboard seed; the brand green is #4cb354 (the most-used green across 295 stylesheets), dark #2e5827; neutral ramps are the grey and slate scales the pages already used.
