Dashboard

The contract price list, but every cell is what the job costs us: machine setup, hooping and handling, sewing at the shop's stitch speed, thread, and the order-level admin cost, divided by the pieces in the order. A price at or above the cell breaks even. Internal only: nothing on this page goes to a customer.

How to read it

  • Break-even cost / pc = (setup + handling × qty + sewing × qty) × hour rate + thread + order cost, all divided by qty. Tier quantities used: 4, 12, 36, 60, 144. A small order carries the setup and order cost on few pieces, which is why the left columns are high.
  • Typical sews on the 8-head machines with no slack. Worst case sews on the smallest machine with the slack percentage on everything, the same model the Volume Quote page uses.
  • Profit / pc at the card = what the customer pays per piece (the card rate × stitches, or the order minimum ÷ qty when the minimum is higher) minus the break-even cost. Red cells lose money.
  • Overhead beyond the order cost (executive, occupancy, card fees) is deliberately not in these cells, matching the July cost model. Change any assumption in Caspio Service_Codes (VOL-* rows) and this page follows on the next load.
