How it works

Approving a design proof
When a new proof is ready you’ll see it under Needs your attention and in Logos & proofs. Click Review & approve to open it, then approve or request changes. Production starts once you approve.
Re-ordering
Go to Your products, open a product, pick the color and sizes, and send it to your rep. Or use Re-order on any past order to start from exactly what you bought. Nothing is charged until you approve a quote.
Turnaround & rush
Standard production is about 7–10 business days after proof approval and garment arrival. Need it sooner? Say so in your request — rush options depend on the job.
Invoices & payment
Every invoice is under Invoices with a downloadable PDF. Balances reflect payments posted in our system. To pay or ask about terms, contact accounting@nwcustomapparel.com or call (253) 922-5793.
Reward dollars
Every order you pay in full earns reward dollars — premium garments earn a higher rate, and seasonal bonuses can multiply it. Rewards post after the invoice is paid; your balance is on the Overview and under Account. To redeem, click Redeem on your next order and tell us the amount: your rep adds it to that order as a credit line, so it shows right on the invoice. Reward dollars never expire while your account stays active.
Logo files & new artwork
Download any approved logo from Logos & proofs. For new artwork, use Send a new logo — your rep replies with where to send the file. Vector (AI, EPS, PDF) or high-resolution PNG works best.
